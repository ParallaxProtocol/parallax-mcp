---

Signing a message proves you control an address without sending anything:
you sign a short text with the account's key, and anyone can check the
signature against the address. Exchanges, pools and other services
sometimes ask for one, as does anyone who wants to know an address is
yours.

Parallax Wallet signs with the standard personal-message signature
(EIP-191, as `personal_sign`), so signatures made here can be checked in
other wallets, and the other way round. Signing a message never moves
funds.

---

## Sign a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sign message">
    Choose **Sign message** in the sidebar. It signs as the account selected
    in the account switcher, shown under **Signing as**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Type the text to sign, for example `I control this address. 2026-10-19`.
    A message is 1 to 1024 bytes of text. Line breaks and tabs are fine;
    other control characters aren't, so what you see is everything that's
    signed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign it">
    Choose **Sign message**. From a hardware wallet account, the device shows
    the message: check it there, then approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    The app shows the address, the message and the signature, each with a
    copy button. **Copy** copies all three together, which is what the other
    party needs to check it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Only sign text you wrote or fully understand. Anyone you give the signature
to can show it as proof that your address signed it.
</Warning>

### From a watch-only account

A watch-only account's key is on your [offline signer](/parallax-wallet/offline-signing),
so the message is signed there:

1. Write the message, then **Create signing request**. The app shows it as
   a QR code, and can save it as a file.
2. On the offline signer, choose **Sign** and scan the code, or open the
   file. Read the whole message, check that the fingerprint matches the one
   your everyday computer shows, then sign it.
3. Back on your everyday computer, **Scan signed message**: hold up the QR
   code the signer shows, or open the file it saved. The app checks that
   it's the message you asked for, and that the signature is from the
   account.

## Verify a message

**Verify message** checks anyone's signed message. It needs no account.

1. Fill in the **message**, the **address** that's meant to have signed it,
   and the **signature**. Or choose **Scan or open** to fill them in from a
   signed message's QR code or file.
2. Choose **Verify**.

The answer is one of:

- **Valid signature**: the address signed exactly this message. If the
  address is one of your accounts or a contact, the app names it.
- **Not signed by this address**: the signature is valid but made by
  another address, which the app shows. Either the address or the message
  isn't what was signed.

The message must match character for character: an added space or line
break changes it.

<Note>
The app takes signatures in the common `0x…` form, and also as the
`parallax-wallet signmessage` command-line tool prints them. It writes them
in the common form.
</Note>
